Original 1K diffuse and OpenGL normal maps (plus roughness for ground scans) are stored locally without image edits. Road scans are tinted in the shader to the median aerial colour sampled along mapped centrelines. Metadata and file SHA-256 checksums are in `sources.json`. The scans repeat at the source's documented physical scale (2.51m and 2.14m). These are representative material samples, not ground surveys of Lyttelton. Geographic surface classes continue to determine where they are used.
